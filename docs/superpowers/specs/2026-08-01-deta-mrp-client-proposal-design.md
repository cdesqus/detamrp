# Deta MRP Client Proposal — Presentation Design

## Purpose

Create a concise English-language sales presentation for prospective clients before a live Deta MRP demonstration. The deck positions Deta MRP as a working, ready-to-use application that can be customized to match the client's business process.

The presentation is intended for a 10–15 minute delivery. It should lead naturally into the live product demo rather than explain every technical detail.

## Audience

Prospective clients, including Directors, management, procurement teams, and operational stakeholders.

## Core Business Context

The proposal addresses a common operational problem:

- Frequent business travel can delay Director approval of paper-based purchase orders.
- Physical documents require manual handovers and repeated follow-up.
- Operational teams have limited visibility into approval status.
- Businesses need a paperless PO process that enables faster online decisions without losing control, accountability, or traceability.

## Positioning

Deta MRP is presented as:

- A working application that is ready to demonstrate.
- A connected platform for procurement and material control.
- A configurable solution that can be customized to fit each company.
- A practical way to accelerate PO approval and improve operational visibility.

The deck must not mention pricing or SAGE integration. It must not imply that every customization is already included or available without discovery.

## Tone and Language

- All slide content is in English.
- Copy is short, confident, conversational, and human.
- The tone is youthful and modern while remaining credible for management.
- Avoid generic transformation language, exaggerated claims, dense paragraphs, and technical jargon.
- Indonesian is used only during internal discussion, never in the final presentation.

## Visual Direction

- White is the dominant background color.
- Light blue is the primary accent color.
- Use generous whitespace and clear visual hierarchy.
- Favor large statements, short supporting copy, simple icons, and clean process diagrams.
- Use real Deta MRP screenshots on the product, approval, module, and demo slides where practical.
- Keep each slide focused on one message.
- Avoid decorative visual clutter, stock-photo-heavy layouts, and overly formal corporate styling.

## Slide Structure and Approved Copy

### Slide 1 — Deta MRP

**A Smarter, Paperless Purchase Order Process**

Simplify approvals. Improve visibility. Keep operations moving.

### Slide 2 — The Current Challenge

**Purchase order approval should not stop when decision-makers are away.**

- Paper documents require physical handovers.
- Business travel can delay approval.
- Teams have limited visibility into PO status.
- Manual follow-ups take time and are difficult to track.

### Slide 3 — What Businesses Need

**A faster process, without losing control.**

- Create and review purchase orders digitally.
- Approve from anywhere.
- Track every PO from submission to completion.
- Keep documents and approval history in one place.
- Give each team access based on their role.

### Slide 4 — Introducing Deta MRP

**One connected workspace for procurement and material control.**

Deta MRP is a ready-to-use platform that digitalizes purchase orders, approvals, receiving, inventory monitoring, and reporting.

The system can be customized to match each company's workflow, roles, and operational requirements.

### Slide 5 — How It Works

**From purchase request to stock visibility.**

Create PO → Submit for Approval → Online Approval → Generate Documents → Receive Materials → Update Inventory → Monitor & Report

Each step is connected, traceable, and accessible from one platform.

### Slide 6 — Paperless Approval

**Approve wherever business takes you.**

- Review PO details online.
- Approve or reject without handling paper documents.
- Receive email notifications for pending approvals.
- Monitor approval status in real time.
- Maintain a complete activity and decision history.

### Slide 7 — Available Modules

**Everything needed to keep the process connected.**

- Dashboard
- Master Data
- Supplier Orders
- Online Approval
- Delivery Documents
- Material Receiving
- Stock Inventory
- Outgoing Materials
- Reports
- Users, Roles & Permissions
- Email and Activity Logs

### Slide 8 — Business Benefits

**Less waiting. Better control. Clearer decisions.**

- Shorter PO approval cycle
- Reduced paper and manual handovers
- Better visibility across teams
- More accurate receiving and inventory records
- Clear ownership and accountability
- Traceable documents and user activities
- A consistent process that is easier to manage

### Slide 9 — Built to Fit Your Process

**Ready today. Customizable for tomorrow.**

1. **Discover** — Understand the current workflow and priorities.
2. **Configure** — Set up users, roles, master data, and approval rules.
3. **Customize** — Adjust forms, documents, and workflows where needed.
4. **Validate** — Test the process together with key users.
5. **Launch** — Train users and support the go-live.

### Slide 10 — Estimated Timeline

**A focused path to implementation.**

| Stage | Estimated Duration |
|---|---:|
| Process Discovery | 1 week |
| Configuration & Customization | 2–3 weeks |
| User Testing & Refinement | 1 week |
| Training & Go-Live Preparation | 1 week |
| Go-Live Support | 1 week |

**Estimated total: 6–7 weeks**

The final timeline will be adjusted based on customization scope and user readiness.

### Slide 11 — See Deta MRP in Action

**Let's walk through the process.**

The live demonstration will show how to:

1. Create a supplier order.
2. Submit it for online approval.
3. Review and approve the PO.
4. Generate the required documents.
5. Receive materials using Kanban scanning.
6. Monitor inventory and transaction history.

**Next step:** Align the system with the prospective client's current process and identify the required customizations.

## Presentation Flow

The presenter opens with the operational pain rather than a feature list. Slides 2–3 establish relevance, slides 4–7 introduce the solution and its coverage, slides 8–10 build the business case and implementation confidence, and slide 11 transitions directly into the product demonstration.

## Live Demo Handoff

The final slide remains visible while the presenter prepares the application. The spoken transition should be brief:

> Rather than adding more slides, let us show you how the process works in Deta MRP.

The demo follows one connected scenario: create a supplier order, submit it, approve it, review the generated documents, receive materials, and inspect the updated inventory and activity history.

## Scope Boundaries

- General-purpose deck; no prospective client name or logo.
- No pricing or commercial quotation.
- No SAGE or accounting integration content.
- No unsupported ROI percentages or performance claims.
- No long technical architecture section.
- No implementation commitment beyond the qualified 6–7 week estimate.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The deck contains 11 slides and can be presented in 10–15 minutes before the live demo.
- All visible presentation copy is in English.
- The visual design uses a white and light-blue palette.
- The proposal clearly communicates the paper-based approval problem and the value of online PO approval.
- Deta MRP is described as available and customizable, not as a concept that must be built from scratch.
- Pricing and SAGE integration are absent.
- The final slide creates a natural transition into the live application demonstration.
